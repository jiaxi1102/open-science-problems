# Why this route matters

The original `5/2` result answers one cover-number question. The exact-threshold route aims at a sharper structural theorem: triangle-free two-colour decompositions of `KG(8,2)` must contain a monochromatic Petersen graph, which then forces an explicit `31/10` fractional-colouring dual certificate. This would explain the obstruction rather than merely enumerate it and would determine the complete two-cover threshold at the first exceptional Kneser instance.
