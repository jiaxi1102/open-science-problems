# Certificate theorem statements

The `petersen` certificate targets:

> Every red/blue colouring of `E(KG(8,2))` with no monochromatic triangle contains a monochromatic induced `KG(5,2)`.

The `petersen-template` certificate targets:

> If the red graph contains the induced `KG(5,2)` on the two-subsets of a five-set `T`, and `S=[8]\T`, then every red-independent family `I` obeys `|I|+|I∩binom(S,2)|≤10`.

These are the only finite statements asserted by the two CNF artifacts.
