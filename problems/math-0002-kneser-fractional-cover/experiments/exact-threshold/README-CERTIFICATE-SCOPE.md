# Certificate scope

The workflow certifies finite propositional statements only. It does not by itself certify:

- the literature-priority claim;
- the general definition or LP duality theorem for fractional chromatic number;
- the cover-to-edge-partition reduction;
- the two-coordinate upper-bound construction;
- the final manuscript wording.

Those bridges are short, explicit mathematical arguments, but they remain separate review obligations until formalized end to end.
