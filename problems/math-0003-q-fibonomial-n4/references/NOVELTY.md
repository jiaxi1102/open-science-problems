# Novelty and prior-art record

## Claim under review

For every `m >= 0`, the q-Fibonomial coefficient

```text
[ m+4 ]
[  4  ]_F
```

has a unimodal coefficient sequence.

## Primary sources

1. N. Bergeron, C. Ceballos, and J. Küstner, *Elliptic and q-Analogs of the Fibonomial Numbers*, SIGMA 16 (2020), Article 076. This introduces the q-Fibonomials and states the general unimodality conjecture.
2. B. B. Connelly, E. Ito, T. C. Martinez, O. Shevchenko, and K. Yang, *Unimodality of q-Fibonomial coefficients for small cases*, arXiv:2605.12822 (2026). This proves the conjecture for lower index `n <= 3`, reports finite computation, and explicitly says the full problem remains open.

## Search performed

The search found the 2020 source paper, the 2026 `n<=3` paper, its computational repository, and mirrors/citations, but no proof of the complete `n=4` case.

## Interpretation

This is evidence of apparent novelty, not a priority certificate. Search indexes can miss unpublished manuscripts, recent revisions, talks, theses, or differently phrased results. The claim must remain `no-prior-proof-found` until independent specialists and the source-paper authors confirm it.
