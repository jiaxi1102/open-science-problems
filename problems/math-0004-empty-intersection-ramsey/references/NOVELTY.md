# Literature and novelty record

Primary source: Heath–McCourt–Parker–Schwieder–Zerbib, *Ramsey Numbers in Kneser Graphs*, arXiv:2510.25734v2 (10 November 2025).

Problem 3 asks for P(2k−1,k); Problem 4 asks for the least ratio beta. Theorem 5 gives beta≤7/3 for k≥12 divisible by six. This investigation gives beta≤2 for every positive k divisible by three. The arXiv record checked in this session listed v2 as the latest version.
