# Original problem

Boštjan Brešar and Douglas F. Rall, **On the isolation numbers in graph products**, arXiv:2608.25752v1 [math.CO], submitted 26 August 2026.

Problem 2 asks whether, for all positive integers `k<n`,

```text
ι(Q_n,Q_k)=γ(Q_{n-k}).
```

The paper proves the lower bound `ι(Q_n,Q_k)≥γ(Q_{n-k})` and leaves the reverse inequality open.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25752
