# Novelty boundary: nine-point link theorem

## Candidate result

The branch proves the finite statement:

> Every red/blue coloring of the triples of a nine-point set with no
> monochromatic perfect matching contains a monochromatic `K_5^(3)`.

Equivalently, for complementary 3-uniform hypergraphs on nine vertices, if
both matching numbers are at most two, then one transversal number is at most
four.

A Fano-star coloring shows that `K_5^(3)` is best possible: no
monochromatic `K_6^(3)` is forced.

## Search completed so far

A targeted search was performed across arXiv and general web indexes using
combinations of:

- `3-uniform hypergraph two-coloring nine vertices perfect matching`;
- `monochromatic K5 perfect matching 3-graph`;
- `complementary 3-graphs matching number two transversal number four`;
- `Ramsey matching critical coloring Fano plane star`;
- `property B partitions of nine points into triples`.

The search did not identify a source stating this exact nine-point implication
or the sharp Fano-star formulation. Results encountered were primarily about
ordinary hypergraph Ramsey numbers, general matching Ramsey numbers, property
B, or unrelated covering/transversal bounds.

## What this search does not establish

This is not a comprehensive priority determination. In particular, the
statement may occur under different terminology in literature on:

- multicolor Ramsey numbers for matchings in uniform hypergraphs;
- critical colorings below the Alon--Frankl--Lovász matching threshold;
- complementary hypergraphs with bounded matching and covering numbers;
- blocking sets, intersecting triple systems, or finite-geometry
  constructions;
- unpublished computations or classification tables.

The finite theorem is therefore labeled a **candidate new lemma**, not a
confirmed novel theorem.

## Required novelty gate

Before public novelty claims:

1. search MathSciNet, zbMATH, Google Scholar, and citation chains around
   hypergraph matching Ramsey critical colorings;
2. ask specialists in extremal set theory or hypergraph Ramsey theory whether
   the implication is known;
3. search for classifications of two-colorings of `K_9^(3)` without a
   monochromatic 3-matching;
4. compare against unpublished or supplementary material associated with the
   Kneser-Ramsey source paper;
5. obtain at least one independent written novelty assessment.

## Current permitted wording

Safe wording:

> We prove and exactly certify a sharp nine-point link lemma that we have not
> located in the targeted search performed so far.

Unsafe wording at the present stage:

> This is the first such theorem, or a new theorem in the literature.
